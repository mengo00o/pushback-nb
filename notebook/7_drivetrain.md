# Drive Train #

**Description**

The VEX game this year values a fast and light drivetrain, which leaves a lot of space for other mechanisms to function. The first step we took to achieve these ideal parameters was to 3D model a prototype of our robot mentioned previously. Our team members modeled many different kinds of robots, but almost always had a similar drive train. This drivetrain is also very similar to last year's ideal drivetrain because of the similar play style of both games.
